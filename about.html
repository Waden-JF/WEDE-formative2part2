Our story

Students helping students.

GreenLoop Campus Pantry is a fictional student-run organisation. It began when a group of students noticed that classmates were finding it hard to afford food while good food was sometimes thrown away.

The pantry collects suitable surplus food from nearby shops, cafés, and campus events. Volunteers sort and pack the food, which is shared with students for free or at a low price.

Our mission: To help students access affordable food while reducing unnecessary food waste on campus.

Our vision: A campus where students can find food support easily and where less good food is wasted.
