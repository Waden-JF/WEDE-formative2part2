Welcome to Greenloop

Affordable food, less food waste.

GreenLoop is a fictional, student-run organisation started by students who saw classmates struggling to afford food while good food was being thrown away. We collect suitable surplus from nearby shops, cafés, and campus events, then share it with students for free or at a low price.
