Volunteer at GreenLoop

Small acts can make a big difference.

Volunteers help the pantry run smoothly and make collection times welcoming for students. You can ask about helping even if you are not sure which role is right for you.

Register your interest

During a shift

Help out your way.

Tasks depend on donations and the needs of the pantry on the day.

01

Sort donations

Help organise suitable surplus food received from local and campus partners.

02

Pack food

Prepare food items and student meal packs for collection.

03

Welcome students

Help visitors find their way and make collection times friendly.

A few expectations

Bring care and a willingness to help.

Please follow food-safety guidance, handle donations carefully, respect everyone’s privacy, and ask the team if you are unsure what to do.
